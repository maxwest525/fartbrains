# Fartbrains — Product Truth

Fartbrains is a **private second brain for one person**. It may be sold to many
customers, but every account is one private personal brain.

## The promise
1. Put anything into Fartbrains.
2. Fartbrains processes and organizes it.
3. Find it later, or ask the brain about it.
4. Everything stays private unless the user deliberately shares one item.

## What this product is NOT
Not a team workspace, company knowledge base, collaborative editor, or
project-management tool. There are no organizations, team accounts, shared
workspaces, member roles, seat-based billing, workspace invitations, shared
vaults, shared folders, or real-time collaborative editing — and none may be
introduced.

## The only interpersonal feature
A user may deliberately share **one specific idea** with a friend through a
secure, revocable, read-only link. The recipient never gains access to the
owner's account, folders, other ideas, graph, conversations, raw vault, or
private metadata. No comments, presence, or shared editing.

## Vocabulary
The database table is `ideas` for historical reasons and is not renamed. The
customer-facing umbrella term is **Library** / **items**, because users capture
thoughts, notes, links, articles, transcripts, lists, research, files, tasks and
project material — not only "ideas".
